## Core Philosophical & Theoretical Bibliography

The Metaphysical Theory Reduction Engine (MTRE) synthesizes concepts across formal logic, dialectics, epistemology, and Robert Lawrence Kuhn’s taxonomy of consciousness theories. Below is the reference catalog underpinning the reduction engine:

### 1. Metaphysics, Dialectics, and Nondual Philosophy
* **Aristotle.** *Metaphysics* and *Prior Analytics*. (Foundational dialectical rules, Law of Non-Contradiction, Principle of Excluded Middle).
* **Kant, Immanuel.** *Critique of Pure Reason* (1781). (Kantian Antinomies, the undecidability of selective/positive conclusions about the world-as-a-whole).
* **Nāgārjuna.** *Mūlamadhyamakakārikā* (*Fundamental Verses on the Middle Way*, ~200 CE). (The *Catuṣkoṭi* four-fold negation deconstructing all positive, one-sided metaphysical assertions).
* **Śaṅkara.** *Brahmasūtra-Bhāṣya* and *Upadeśasahasrī* (8th Century CE). (Advaita Vedānta formulation of apophatic non-dual awareness and the illusion of subject-object separation).

### 2. Kuhn's Consciousness Landscape & Taxonomies
* **Kuhn, Robert Lawrence.** "A Landscape of Consciousness Theories." *Closer to Truth* / *Progress in Biophysics and Molecular Biology* (2024). 
  * Provides the universal 10-category taxonomy:
    1. Materialism Theories
    2. Non-reductive Physicalism
    3. Quantum Theories
    4. Integrated Information Theory (IIT)
    5. Panpsychisms
    6. Monisms
    7. Dualisms
    8. Idealisms
    9. Anomalous and Altered States Theories
    10. Challenge Theories

### 3. Contemporary Theories of Consciousness Cited in the System
* **Chalmers, David J.** *The Conscious Mind: In Search of a Fundamental Theory*. Oxford University Press, 1996. (Formulation of the "Hard Problem" and Property Dualism).
* **Dennett, Daniel C.** *Consciousness Explained*. Little, Brown and Co., 1991. (Reductive Physicalism / Eliminativism).
* **Goff, Philip.** *Galileo's Error: Foundations for a New Science of Consciousness*. Pantheon Books, 2019. (Constitutive Micro-Panpsychism).
* **Hoffman, Donald D.** *The Case Against Reality: How Evolution Hid the Truth from Our Eyes*. W. W. Norton & Company, 2019. (User-Interface Realism / Conscious Realism).
* **Kastrup, Bernardo.** *The Idea of the World: A Multi-Disciplinary Argument for the Mental Nature of Reality*. Iff Books, 2019. (Analytic Idealism).
* **Penrose, Roger, and Stuart Hameroff.** "Consciousness in the Universe: A Review of the 'Orch OR' Theory." *Physics of Life Reviews*, vol. 11, no. 1, 2014, pp. 39–78. (Quantum Microtubule Reduction).
* **Tononi, Giulio, et al.** "Integrated Information Theory (IIT) 4.0: Formulating the Properties of Phenomenal Existence in Physical Terms." *PLOS Computational Biology*, 2023. (Informational Realism / $\Phi$).
